# Changelog

Notable changes to this list. Typo fixes and small wording tweaks are not logged.

The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-05

Initial public-ready release.

### Added

- 106 curated research tools across 13 categories, each with an official link, a
  written one-sentence description, and controlled-vocabulary pricing and platform labels.
- Categories: AI search and answer engines, Deep research agents, Academic search engines, Paper discovery and citation mapping, Reading and summarising papers, Literature review and evidence synthesis, Citation and reference management, Knowledge management and notes, Data and statistical research, Market and competitive research, User and qualitative research, Fact checking and verification, and Research APIs and open infrastructure.
- Contribution files: `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` (Contributor Covenant 2.1),
  `SECURITY.md`.
- `LICENSE` — CC BY 4.0, canonical text.
- Issue forms for suggesting a tool and reporting a broken link, plus a pull
  request template and issue template configuration.
- `.editorconfig`, `.gitattributes`, and `.gitignore`.

### Notes

- Every official URL was checked before release. Entries whose product had been
  discontinued, absorbed, or renamed were corrected or dropped rather than carried over.
- All outbound links are clean canonical URLs. No affiliate or referral parameters.
- Licensed under CC BY 4.0. A content licence rather than a software licence, because this repository is prose and links.
